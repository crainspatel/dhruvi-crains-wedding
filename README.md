Dhruvi & Crains — On-Scroll Photo Formations
=============================================

Upload *the contents of this folder* to the root of your GitHub Pages repository.

This version uses GSAP ScrollTrigger with an isolated CSS photo grid, inspired by
Codrops' MIT-licensed OnScrollLayoutFormations demo:
https://github.com/codrops/OnScrollLayoutFormations

On desktop, the memories start stacked at the center and scatter into a complete
seven-photo collage as you scroll; the gallery holds its final shape. On phones,
photos reveal in a readable two-column layout. The five additional photos follow
in a separate gallery.

Images are actual supplied photos, not AI generated. Our Story text is unchanged.
Family schedules and maps are unchanged.

NOTE: Animations require GSAP and ScrollTrigger downloaded from jsdelivr on the
visitor's browser. If they fail to load, the static photo gallery stays visible.
For a production launch, consider self-hosting verified copies of those libraries.
